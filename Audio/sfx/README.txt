Sound effects. Drop a file here named after the sound (.ogg, .wav or .mp3; letter case doesn't matter) and the
game uses it instead of the placeholder made in code (Scripts/autoload/sfx_synth.gd). No code changes needed.

Battle sounds:
  swing      a front-row fighter lunges in (also assists, pitched up)
  shoot      a back-row fighter fires a bolt
  puke       Roki's horse form attacks
  hit        an attack lands (also bleed/poison ticks, quieter and lower)
  crit       a critical hit
  miss       an attack misses
  heal       a heal or revive
  knockout   a friend or enemy goes down
  ultimate   an Ultimate or combo cut-in starts
  wave       a new wave's banner
  jumpscare  a boss wave's lights-out entrance
  victory    results screen after a win (or a world boss fight)
  defeat     results screen after a loss

Recruit sounds:
  crystal    a crystal lands in the x10 lineup (pitched up for SR / SSR colours)
  ultimate   (also) a lineup crystal upgrading to a higher tier, pitched up

Keep them short and trimmed (no silence at the start). Several hits can play at once, so leave some headroom.
